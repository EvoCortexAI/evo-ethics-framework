# Licensing Decision Required

Public visibility does not itself grant reuse rights.

## Recommended model (pending approval)

- **Code:** Apache License 2.0 — explicit patent grant and broad implementation rights.
- **Normative documentation and specifications:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

This proposal intentionally does not include final license texts. Founder approval and legal review are required before public publication.

## Gate

Until the model above (or an alternative) is approved and the final texts are committed:

- The proprietary license in `LICENSE` remains in force.
- The repository must stay private.
- No public claim of open-source status may be made.

See `docs/PUBLIC-RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md` for the full set of gates.
